# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and evidence-oriented community. Examples of positive behavior include:

- Being considerate and constructive.
- Welcoming different perspectives and levels of experience.
- Giving specific, actionable feedback.
- Correcting errors transparently.
- Focusing discussion on projects, evidence, and the quality of the list.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, deliberate misinformation, spam, undisclosed paid promotion, and publishing another person's private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior is inconsistent with these standards.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact options on the [GitHub profile](https://github.com/yamancan). Reports will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as practical.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, and other spaces where someone is representing the project.
