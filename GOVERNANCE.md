# Governance

Awesome Stellar AI is a community-curated technical resource. Its job is to help builders find credible agent infrastructure and applications on Stellar without turning the repository into a complete project directory or a marketing channel.

## Editorial Principles

- **Evidence over claims:** Material statements must be independently verifiable.
- **Relevance over affiliation:** Stellar and AI agents must both be substantive parts of the work.
- **Curation over collection:** A smaller useful list is better than an exhaustive weak one.
- **Primary sources first:** Repositories, technical documentation, releases, deployments, and on-chain records are preferred over announcements.
- **Precise maturity:** Experimental, testnet, mainnet, hosted, and published are different claims.
- **One primary category:** Projects are grouped by the capability they primarily provide.
- **Correction over permanence:** Inclusion can be revisited when evidence or project status changes.

## Decision Process

1. A submission identifies the project, its primary category, and the way it uses Stellar and AI agents.
2. The submitter provides direct evidence for deployment, release, network, and standards-integration claims.
3. A reviewer checks the public sources, wording, category, duplicates, and relationship disclosure.
4. The README and `catalog/evidence.json` are updated together.
5. Automated checks verify structure, ordering, catalog coverage, and link health.
6. The final decision is recorded in the pull request history.

There is no automatic right to inclusion. Maintainers may request narrower wording, stronger evidence, a different category, or more project maturity.

## Conflicts of Interest

Maintainers, grant recipients, investors, advisers, hackathon organizers, and project contributors may propose affiliated work, but they must disclose the relationship. Affiliated projects receive the same evidence and distinctiveness review as unaffiliated projects.

When practical, a maintainer should not be the only reviewer of an affiliated addition. If only one maintainer is available, the relationship and supporting evidence must be explicit in the pull request so the decision remains auditable.

## Maintenance

The catalog is reviewed on a rolling basis and receives a full evidence pass at least every 120 days. Link health runs weekly. Entries with broken links, missing evidence, archived sources, misleading maturity claims, or no remaining distinct value may be corrected or removed.

The Git history is the decision log. Material additions, removals, category changes, and policy changes should use pull requests once the repository is public.

## Independence

Awesome Stellar AI is independent and is not maintained by or on behalf of the Stellar Development Foundation. References to official Stellar resources are labeled through their source, not used to imply endorsement of this list or any listed project.
